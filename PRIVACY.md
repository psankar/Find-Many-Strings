# Privacy Policy for Find Many Strings

_Last updated: November 19, 2026_

Find Many Strings is a browser extension that highlights several search terms at once on the web page you are viewing.

## Summary

Find Many Strings does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal or browsing data.

## What the extension accesses

The extension does nothing until you click its toolbar button. When you do, it accesses only the tab you are viewing:

- **Selected text:** if text is selected on the page, it is copied into the popup's search box so you don't have to retype it.
- **Page text:** the page's text is searched for your terms, and matches are highlighted on that page.

## What happens to that data

- Your search terms and the page content stay in your browser, on that page.
- The extension makes no network requests. It sends nothing to the developer or to anyone else.
- The extension saves nothing. It uses no browser storage, cookies, or analytics. The state of the current search (the terms, their colors, and your position among the matches) is held in memory on that page only. It is discarded when you clear the highlights, reload the page, or close the tab.

## Permissions

- **activeTab:** lets the extension access the current tab, and only after you click the toolbar button.
- **scripting:** lets the extension run its own bundled highlighting code on that tab. No code is downloaded from the internet.

The extension cannot access your other tabs, and it never runs on pages in the background.

Any changes will be published in this file, and the date above will be updated.
