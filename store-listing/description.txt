Find Many Strings highlights several words or phrases at once on any web page, each in its own color, so you can see where all of them appear in one pass.

Type your search terms separated by commas (or any separator you choose) and click Search. Every match is highlighted, and the popup lists each term with its number of matches. Use the ▲ ▼ buttons next to a term to jump to its previous or next match. The current match is outlined and scrolled into view.

Useful for:
• Scanning long articles, contracts or documentation for several keywords
• Reading logs for errors, IDs and service names at the same time
• Checking a recipe, product page or report for specific items

Features:
• Highlight many terms in one search (matching ignores upper and lower case)
• Distinct, readable colors that work on both light and dark websites
• Step through the matches of each term separately, with a match counter
• Text you select on the page is filled in as your search
• The popup follows your browser's light or dark theme
• Clear all highlights with one click

Privacy:
Find Many Strings collects no data. It works only on the tab you are viewing, and only after you click its button. Nothing is stored or sent anywhere. It uses only the "activeTab" and "scripting" permissions.

New in version 2.0:

• Previous/next navigation for each search term
• New popup design with light and dark themes
